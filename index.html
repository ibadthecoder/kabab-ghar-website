About

Two branches in Hyderabad

The first Kabab Ghar is on Wadhu Wah Road in Qasimabad, behind Sunny CNG Pump. The second is at Mehran Walk Food Street on Auto Bhan Road.

The menu goes from grill fish and mutton dumphukt to handi biryani, nachos and loaded fries, then shakes, mocktails and ice cream desserts. Prices change, so message us for today’s menu.
